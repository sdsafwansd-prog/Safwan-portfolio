# SAFWAN SD - Data Analytics Portfolio Website

An ultra-modern, responsive, high-performance portfolio website built specifically for **SAFWAN SD**, **Data Analytics Specialist & Business Intelligence Professional**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Personal Profile & Photo Showcase**:
   - Modern portrait card with glowing interactive badges (Python & SQL, Power BI & Tableau, ETL & Warehousing).
   - Instant in-browser photo preview tool with persistent local storage.
   - Built-in SVG fallback avatar.

2. **Interactive Live Analytics Dashboard (Chart.js)**:
   - Live data visualization demo directly on the page showcasing your skills to hiring managers.
   - Switchable metrics:
     - **Revenue & Forecast** (Time-series actual vs predicted revenue)
     - **Churn & Retention** (Cohort retention & churn analysis)
     - **Category & Channels** (Contribution distribution doughnut chart)
   - Interactive segment filters & simulated real-time ETL data refresh button.

3. **Curated Analytics Case Studies**:
   - Filterable projects by technology: *Python & ML*, *Power BI & Tableau*, *SQL & Warehousing*, *Excel & Modeling*.
   - Deep-dive interactive modal breakdowns with business problems, technical architecture, and measurable outcomes.

4. **Technical Competency Matrix**:
   - Categorized Bento grid covering Languages, BI tools, Databases & Cloud, and Statistical Methodologies.

5. **Experience & Certifications Timeline**:
   - Highlights your practical analytics experience, Google Data Analytics certificate, Microsoft Power BI certification (PL-300), and SQL credentials.

6. **Lead Generation & Contact Form**:
   - Functional contact form with validation, social links (LinkedIn, GitHub, Email), and location badges.

7. **Themes & Performance**:
   - Dark Mode (default data-terminal aesthetic) and crisp Light Mode toggle.
   - 100% responsive across mobile, tablet, and widescreen desktop displays.
   - Zero bulky framework dependencies—lightning fast load times.

## 🚀 How to Run & Preview Your Portfolio

### Option 1: Double Click
Simply double-click `index.html` in Windows Explorer or open it in Google Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.

### Option 2: Run a Local Server (Recommended)
Open PowerShell inside `c:\portfolio 2` and run:
```powershell
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then open your browser and navigate to:
[http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000)

---

## 🖼️ How to Add Your Picture

1. Select your photo (JPG, PNG, or WEBP).
2. Save it as:
   ```
   c:\portfolio 2\assets\profile.jpg
   ```
3. Refresh your website, and your photo will appear in the glowing profile frame!
*(You can also click the camera button on your website in the browser to choose a picture immediately).*
